Fatty Liver Food List

Eat Freely

  • Vegetables (Daily - Wide Variety)
  • Spinach, Broccoli
  • Kale, Carrots
  • Beetroot, Cabbage
  • Cauliflower
  • Brussels sprouts
  • Zucchini
  • Bell peppers, Asparagus
  • Garlic
  • Onions, Cucumber
  • Leafy greens (all types)
  • Fruits
  • Apples
  • Berries (blueberries, strawberries, raspberries)
  • Oranges, Grapefruit
  • Lemon & lime, Pomegranate
  • Papaya & Kiwi
  • Pears
  • Watermelon (small portions)
  • Whole Grains (High Fiber Choices)
  • Oats, Brown rice
  • Quinoa
  • Whole wheat
  • Barley, Millet
  • Buckwheat
  • Whole grain bread (no added sugar)
  • Lean Protein (Supports Repair)
  • Chicken breast & Turkey
  • Fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel)
  • Eggs (boiled)
  • Lentils, Chickpeas
  • Beans (black beans, kidney beans)
  • Tofu, Low-fat yogurt
  • Cottage cheese (low-fat)
  • Healthy Fats (Reduce Inflammation)
  • Avocado
  • Olive oil (extra virgin)
  • Nuts (almonds, walnuts, pistachios)
  • Seeds (chia, flax, sunflower, pumpkin)
  • Fatty fish (omega-3 rich)
  • Drinks (Low sugar)
  • Green tea, Black coffee (no sugar)
  • Lemon water
  • Herbal teas (ginger, turmeric, chamomile)
  • Coconut water (unsweetened)
  • Plenty of water (8-10 glasses daily)

Limit

  • Refined Carbohydrates (Low Fiber)
  • White bread
  • White rice
  • Pasta (regular)
  • Noodles
  • Breakfast cereals (refined)
  • Crackers
  • Pancakes & waffles
  • High-Fat Dairy (Use Sparingly)
  • Butter
  • Cheese (especially processed)
  • Cream
  • Full-fat milk
  • Flavored yogurt (high sugar)
  • Natural Sugars (Small Amounts Only)
  • Honey, Maple syrup
  • Fruit juices (even fresh)
  • Smoothies with added sugar
  • Dried fruits (raisins, dates - very small portions)
  • Red Meat (Limit Weekly)
  • Beef, Lamb
  • Goat meat, Pork
  • High Sodium Foods
  • Packaged soups
  • Sauces (soy sauce, ketchup)
  • Pickles
  • Instant noodles
  • Salted snacks
  • Caffeinated / Add-On Drinks
  • Coffee with sugar/cream
  • Flavored lattes
  • Energy drinks
  • Sweetened tea
  • Processed "Healthy" Foods (Hidden Risks)
  • Granola bars
  • Protein bars (added sugar)
  • Low-fat packaged foods (often high sugar)

Avoid

  • Fried & Fast Foods
  • French fries, Fried chicken
  • Burgers
  • Pizza (greasy, processed cheese)
  • Fried snacks (samosa, pakora, tempura)
  • Onion rings, Fried street food
  • Sugar & Sweetened Foods
  • Soft drinks / soda
  • Packaged fruit juices
  • Candy, toffees
  • Cakes, pastries, donuts
  • Ice cream, Milk chocolate
  • Syrups (corn syrup, glucose syrup)
  • Alcohol
  • Beer, Wine, Whiskey
  • Vodka, Rum
  • Cocktails
  • Processed Meats
  • Sausages, Bacon
  • Salami, Hot dogs
  • Pepperoni, Deli meats
  • Ultra-Processed Foods
  • Instant noodles
  • Frozen ready meals
  • Packaged soups
  • Frozen fried items
  • Boxed meal kits
  • High Sodium Foods
  • Chips & salted snacks
  • Instant soups
  • Pickles
  • Sauces (soy sauce, ketchup)
  • Canned processed foods
  • Unhealthy Fats & Oils
  • Palm oil

  • Reused frying oil

  • Refined Carbohydrates
  • White rice (large amounts)
